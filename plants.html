For plants

Seed library, season planning and plant breeding in one place.

From knowing what seed you have, to saving your own, to running a breeding project over many generations. Tap any feature to see how it works and where to find it.

Getting started

Seed library from packet photosKnow exactly what seed you have.All modes

Photograph a packet and it goes into your library with the amount, year and best-before date. With a free Gemini key (or a Claude key), the name, species and source are read from the photo for you to check.

Where: + › Scan a packet or label

Check before buyingStanding in the shop? See if you already have it.All modes

Type the name on the packet. It finds close matches like "Money Maker" for "Moneymaker", shows how much you have and how old it is, and warns about F1 hybrids. Works without internet.

Where: Home › Check before buying

Season planList what you'll grow, and spot problems early.All modes

Pick varieties for the season. It checks you have enough seed, that it's not too old, whether you mean to save seed from an F1, and which varieties could cross. Tapping Sown takes the seed off the packet.

Where: My garden › Season plan

Seed traysA tray ID, cell positions, then pot up the survivors.All modes

Each tray gets an ID like T01 and each cell a position like A1. Tap cells as they come up to see the germination rate, then pot up the ones you keep as labelled plants.

Where: Open a packet › Sow a tray

Simple or detailedOnly see what you need.All modes

Choose Garden, Seed saver or Breeder, and switch single features on or off. Nothing you record is lost when you change.

Where: Settings › Mode

Growing

My garden pageEverything you set up, in one place.All modes

The fence button at the top of the screen opens My garden: beds, weather, inputs, harvests, varieties and packets, season plan, seed trays, labels and backups, each with a count and a quick add button.

Where: Top of the screen › My garden

Your plantsEvery plant that matters gets its own record.All modes

Each plant gets a label code, a photo timeline and notes. Codes come from the variety's initials, like RDN-P01 for Ronde de Nice.

Where: Plants tab › Add a plant

Printable labels with QR codesStake labels that open the plant's record.Seed saver and Breeder

Print stake, sheet or packet-sized labels. Scanning the QR code with a phone camera opens that plant or packet in the app.

Where: Plants tab › Labels

Fertiliser and spraysWhat went on, how much, and when it's safe to harvest.All modes

Set up each fertiliser or spray once with the label rate and withholding period. Log it against one plant, a whole bed, a tray or a project. It shows "don't harvest until" dates, repeat reminders such as a fortnightly feed, and season totals.

Where: My garden › Inputs

Beds, pots and greenhousesSet up where things grow.All modes

Record where each plant grows, compare how beds perform, and log an input or a harvest for everything in a bed at once.

Where: My garden › Beds, pots and greenhouses

Weather by seasonHow this season compares with a usual one.All modes

Add your location to see rain, heat days, frost nights and growing heat against the usual year, alongside your results. Add your own rain gauge readings or import a weather station file.

Where: My garden › Weather

HarvestsWhat each bed and plant produces.All modes

Set up what you grow once, such as tomatoes in kilograms or pumpkins by count and weight. Record harvests against a plant, a bed or a tray, and see season totals next to the fertiliser and sprays used.

Where: + › Record a harvest

Due listWhat needs doing next.All modes

Worked out from your records: trays to check, fruit set after hand pollination, storage checks, germination tests, feeds and your own reminders.

Where: Home › Due

Saving seed and breeding

Seed-saving guidanceHow each crop pollinates and how far apart to keep varieties.Seed saver and Breeder

For about 25 common crops: whether it pollinates itself or needs insects or wind, isolation distances, and how many plants to save seed from.

Where: Open a variety

Germination testsFind out if old seed is still good.Seed saver and Breeder

Count sprouts over a few days. It works out the rate and tells you when a packet needs regrowing.

Where: Open a seed packet › Germination tests

Crosses and hand pollinationRecord every cross and how many took.Seed saver and Breeder

Record the parents, method, attempts and results. You get a reminder to check fruit set, and each cross can get a code like 26C-1 to write on the flower tag.

Where: Open a plant › Cross

Cuttings and clonesPropagate a plant and keep the link to it.All modes

Record cuttings, divisions, runners or grafts as one batch or as separate plants. Clones share their source plant's family line.

Where: Open a plant › Propagate

Breeding projects and goalsWhat you're breeding towards, with history.Breeder

Write the goal, what's in and out of scope and what success looks like. Every revision is kept with the reason, so you can see how the project evolved.

Where: Projects tab › New project

Selection scoresScore candidates against your targets.Breeder

Score each plant 1 to 5 on your target traits, compare them side by side, and step through a whole family quickly at harvest.

Where: Open a project › Scoring session

Bloodline chartA family tree of the whole project.Breeder

See where every plant came from and how much of each original variety it carries. Tap one to trace its ancestors and descendants.

Where: Open a project › Bloodline chart

Cross planner and progressPlan the next crosses and see if selection is working.Breeder

Plan crosses ahead of flowering, see success rates by pairing, and compare average and best scores from one generation to the next.

Where: Open a project › Cross planner

Sharing

Share with a friendOffer spare seed, with its history.All modes

Makes a picture and message to send. Optionally attach a gift file your friend can open in their own Lineage Tracker, so the variety's story goes with the seed.

Where: Open a variety, packet or plant › Share

Selling and swappingPrices, postage and a catalogue.Off until you turn it on

List seeds, cuttings or young plants with a price (or free or swap) and postage. Share single offers, a catalogue picture or a text list for Facebook groups. Lineage Tracker never handles payment or adds your personal details.

Where: Settings › Selling and swapping

Provenance sheetsA printable record for seed you pass on.Seed saver and Breeder

Shows the variety, parents, cross, germination tests and whether it was hand pollinated.

Where: Open a packet › Provenance

History reportOne plant's full story, for an adviser or friend.All modes

Choose the dates and what to include, then save a printable report as a PDF, copy a short summary, or download a spreadsheet.

Where: Open a plant › History

Your records

BackupsEverything in one file.All modes

One tap makes a file with every record and photo, plus spreadsheet copies you can open without the app.

Where: Settings › Back up now

Sync across devicesPhone, tablet and computer in step.All modes

Syncs through your own Google Drive, with no Lineage Tracker server in between. Add a passphrase to encrypt everything before it leaves your device.

Where: Settings › Sync across devices

Workspaces and shared librariesExamples, friends' libraries and your own lists.Seed saver and Breeder

Examples open separately so they never mix with your records. Open a library another breeder shares, or split your records into lists, and copy items between them.

Where: Settings › Workspaces

Import old recordsBring in spreadsheets and notebooks.Breeder

Import CSV spreadsheets, or records converted by Claude or ChatGPT using the guide. You see a preview first, and one tap undoes it.

Where: Settings › Import

Free, and your records stay yours

No account, no ads, no subscription. Everything is stored on your own device, works without internet, and can be backed up in one tap.
