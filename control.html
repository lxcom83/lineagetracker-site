You're in control

The app works fully offline. Anything that uses the internet is a switch you turn on yourself.

The Lineage Tracker app needs no account and no connection. Every connected feature below is off until you switch it on, and you can switch it off again at any time. Behind the cloud button at the top of the app you will find them all in one place, with Work offline to pause everything at once.

The switches

  • Sync between your devices. Keeps your records, and optionally your photos, in step through your own Google Drive or Dropbox, in a folder only Lineage Tracker can see. There is no Lineage Tracker server in between. You can protect the synced copy with a passphrase.
  • Sharing your AI key between your devices. Only available once sync is protected with a passphrase. Your Gemini or Claude key is encrypted with that passphrase and kept in your own storage, so you only type it once. Switching it off on one device switches it off on the others.
  • Smart label reading and questions. Sends the label photo or question you choose to Google Gemini or Claude, using your own key. Nothing is sent without you pressing the button, and you can choose copy-and-paste with a Claude or ChatGPT app instead.
  • Weather for your sites. Looks up weather for the sites you add.
  • App updates. The app checks for a newer version of itself.
  • Club version check. Only in a partner version of the app: once a day it asks Lineage Tracker whether that version is still switched on. Nothing about you or your records is sent.

What never needs a switch

  • Recording, searching, printing, exporting and backing up your records.
  • Sharing a picture or text: you press Share, you choose where it goes, and you can edit the words first.

The website tools

The tools at tools.lineagetracker.org (such as LT-Print and LT-Planner) are separate from the app and need a free website account. The app never does. See Your data, your choice for what we keep.
