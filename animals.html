For animals

Pedigrees, pairings, hatch dates and health records.

For poultry, goats, rabbits and other small-scale breeding: every animal, its family line, its care and its offspring. Tap any feature to see how it works and where to find it.

Getting started

Breeds and stockBreeds, hatching eggs, young and adult animals.All modes

Keep each breed with its source, and track hatching eggs or young stock with amounts and dates.

Where: Library › Variety or breed

Flocks and groupsA pen of hens as one entry.All modes

Record a flock or pen as a group, so feed, worming and egg collection are logged once. Pick out a bird to track for breeding any time.

Where: Animals tab › Add a group

Your animalsEach animal with its own record and IDs.All modes

Every animal gets a code, photo timeline and notes, plus ring or band, microchip and registration numbers.

Where: Animals tab › Add an animal

My farm pageEverything you set up, in one place.All modes

The fence button at the top of the screen opens My farm: pens and paddocks, weather, medicines and feeds, products like eggs and milk, breeds, reminders and backups, each with a count and a quick add button.

Where: Top of the screen › My farm

Simple or detailedOnly see what you need.All modes

Choose Animals in Settings to hide plant-only features, then pick Garden, Seed saver or Breeder.

Where: Settings › Mode

Breeding

PairingsRecord matings and how they went.Seed saver and Breeder

Record sire and dam, natural mating or AI, attempts and results.

Where: Open an animal › Cross

Birth and hatch due datesExpected dates worked out for you.All modes

Works out due dates for about 17 kinds of livestock and poultry. For eggs, add the date they were set to get reminders to candle at day 7 and stop turning three days before hatch.

Where: Record a pairing › Due list

Litters, weigh-ins and egg logsProduction records you can compare.Breeder (or switch on Projects in Settings)

Add record types from templates: litters, weigh-ins, egg logs (per hen or per pen) and show results. Compare animals side by side.

Where: Open a project › Record types

Inbreeding checksKnow the inbreeding before you pair.Breeder

Shows the inbreeding figure for each animal and for any planned pairing, with green, amber and red levels, and suggests the lowest-inbreeding partner for each female.

Where: Open a project › Cross planner

Bloodline chartYour line's family tree.Breeder

See where every animal came from and how much of each founder it carries. Tap one to trace its ancestors and descendants.

Where: Open a project › Bloodline chart

Pedigree certificatesPrintable three-generation pedigrees.Seed saver and Breeder

Includes registration, ring and microchip numbers, the inbreeding figure, your breeder name and a QR code linking to the record.

Where: Open an animal › Pedigree

Clutches, litters and hatchesEggs set, fertile, hatched, or born and weaned.Seed saver and Breeder

Record a hatch or birth from a pairing and the percentages are worked out. Then add the chicks or kids in one tap, each with its tag and both parents, or as a group until they're tagged.

Where: Open a pairing › Record a hatch or birth

Colour genetics calculatorWhat colours will the chicks be?Breeder

Pick the genes and each parent, and see the expected offspring, like 50% blue, 25% black, 25% splash. Handles sex-linked traits like barring, and saves the prediction to a planned pairing.

Where: Open a project › Colour genetics calculator

Goals and selectionBreed towards a written goal.Breeder

Set a goal with target traits, score animals against it, and see progress from one generation to the next.

Where: Projects tab › New project

Care

Medicines, wormers and feedsTreatments with withholding periods.All modes

Set up each product once with its withholding period before eggs, milk or meat. Log it for one animal or a whole pen. It shows "no eggs until" dates, repeat reminders such as worming every three months, and season totals.

Where: My farm › Inputs

Health recordsVaccinations, treatments, illness and injuries.Breeder (or switch on Projects in Settings)

Keep a dated health record for each animal, alongside its notes and photos.

Where: Open a project › Record types

Eggs, milk and other productsProduction records you can compare.All modes

Set up what you collect once, such as eggs, milk or honey. Record it from one animal or a whole pen, and see season totals beside the feed and treatments given. Handy for comparing laying with friends.

Where: + › Record eggs, milk or other products

History for the vetEverything about one animal, in one report.All modes

Choose the dates and what to include, then save a printable report as a PDF, copy a summary into a message, or download a spreadsheet.

Where: Open an animal › History

Coops, pens and paddocksWhere each animal lives.All modes

Record housing, compare setups, and log a treatment or collect eggs for everything in a pen at once.

Where: My farm › Coops, pens and paddocks

Due listWhat needs doing next.All modes

Hatch and birth dates, candling, treatments and boosters due, withholding periods ending, and your own reminders.

Where: Home › Due

Sharing

Share an animalA ready-made post for social media.All modes

Makes a picture card and caption for any animal or project, to show off your line.

Where: Open an animal › Share

Selling and swappingHatching eggs and young stock, with prices.Off until you turn it on

Make a list of what you have, with prices (or free or swap) and postage or pick-up, and share it as a catalogue file, PDF, web page or text list. Facebook Marketplace doesn't allow animal sales, so check each group's rules. Lineage Tracker never handles payment.

Where: Settings, Selling and swapping › then Lists

Your records

BackupsEverything in one file.All modes

One tap makes a file with every record and photo, plus spreadsheet copies you can open without the app.

Where: Settings › Back up now

Sync across devicesPhone, tablet and computer in step.All modes

Syncs through your own Google Drive or Dropbox, with no Lineage Tracker server in between. Add a passphrase to encrypt everything before it leaves your device.

Where: Settings › Sync across devices

Workspaces and shared librariesKeep lines separate, or open a friend's.Seed saver and Breeder

Split your records into lists such as "Chickens" and "Goats", open a library another breeder shares, and copy animals between them with their family line.

Where: Settings › Workspaces

Import old recordsBring in spreadsheets and herd books.Breeder

Import CSV spreadsheets with columns like Dam and Sire, or records converted by Claude or ChatGPT. You see a preview first, and one tap undoes it.

Where: Settings › Import

Free, and your records stay yours

No account, no ads, no subscription. Everything is stored on your own device, works without internet, and can be backed up in one tap.
