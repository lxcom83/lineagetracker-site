Pick a region

Tap a glowing dot on the globe, search for a town, or choose from the list below. You will see the local groups and what is grown or bred there. The globe never shows names, addresses or contact details.

How it works
  • Anyone can look around. You don't need an account to browse.
  • Members choose whether to appear, and only as a region, never an address.
  • To get in touch with someone, sign in (it's free). They decide whether to say yes.
  • We point you to the clubs, groups and forums that already exist. The conversation happens there.
