Your data, your choice

Your records belong to you.

Lineage Tracker is built on one idea: the records of your plants, animals and breeding work belong to you. They stay on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you decide it should.

Our promises

  • Your records stay on your device. There's no Lineage Tracker server holding your data.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking in the app. The app doesn't collect analytics or follow what you do.
  • You can take everything with you. Backups include every record and photo, plus spreadsheet copies, in an open, documented format.
  • Sync uses your own Google Drive, and can only see the files Lineage Tracker creates. Add a passphrase and everything is encrypted before it leaves your device.
  • Smart features are off unless you turn them on, and the app tells you exactly what will be sent.
  • You share only what you choose. Sharing, gift files and listings contain only what you pick, and never payment details or personal information you didn't type.
  • Anything online in future will be opt-in, item by item.
  • It's free. We never sell data, and there are no paid tiers.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you record is stored in your browser's storage on your device.

When information leaves your device

Only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Sync (optional): your records and photos are copied to your own Google Drive, using permission that only covers files Lineage Tracker creates. With a passphrase, they're encrypted first.
  • Smart features (optional): if you add a Google Gemini or Claude key, a packet or label photo, or a question you ask, is sent to that company to be answered. Their terms apply. On Google's free tier, Google may use what's sent to improve its products.
  • Weather (optional): the location of a site you add is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch weather.
  • Sharing: pictures, messages, gift files and offers go only where you send them, using your own apps.
  • Feedback: what you type in the feedback form is sent through Web3Forms, which emails it to us. Emails to feedback@lineagetracker.org come straight to us.
  • Fonts: the app and website load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your device's internet address, as with most websites.
  • Website visits: lineagetracker.org (the website, not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies, with Google's ad features switched off. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker.
  • Hosting: the app and website are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic visit logs for security.
  • Partner versions: if you use a version offered by a club, seed library or business, its logo and any variety list load from their website. They can't see your records.

Children

Lineage Tracker doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions are welcome through the feedback form or by email to feedback@lineagetracker.org.
