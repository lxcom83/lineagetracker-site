Make it yours

Your club's own app, free.

Seed libraries, garden clubs, breed societies, schools and nurseries can offer members their own version of Lineage Tracker, with your name, logo, colours and the features that suit you. It takes a few minutes. There's nothing to install or host, and you never need to ask us.

  • Your brand: name, logo, colours and a welcome message.
  • Your focus: plants, animals or both, and only the features your members need.
  • Your varieties: optionally, link a list so members start with your catalogue.
  • Always up to date: your version runs on the official app, so improvements reach your members automatically.
  • Private: members' records stay on their own devices. You don't see them, and neither do we.

The terms

  1. Use the kit. Your version runs from the official Lineage Tracker app. You may not copy, host or modify the code.
  2. Keep the credit. Your version shows "Powered by Lineage Tracker", and things people share from it say so.
  3. Keep the support links. Donation and support links go to Lineage Tracker. You can still ask members to support your own organisation separately.
  4. Don't charge for it. You may not charge your members or customers for access to your version.
  5. Use your own name and logo, and only variety lists you have the right to share. Don't suggest you are Lineage Tracker.
  6. Respect privacy. Members' records stay on their own devices. Don't ask them to send you their records unless they choose to.
  7. You're responsible for what you add: your welcome message, your variety list and any claims about your products.
  8. Withdrawal. Lineage Tracker may withdraw a version that breaks these terms or is misleading or offensive. Members keep their records and continue with plain Lineage Tracker.
Need more? Your own web address, custom features, or your own branding without the Lineage Tracker credit are available under a paid licence. Email feedback@lineagetracker.org.
