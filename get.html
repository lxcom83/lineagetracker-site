Install it

There is no app store. You open the link in your web browser, tap one button, and the app sits on your home screen or desktop like any other app. Do this once. After that it works without internet.

Menus change a little between versions and phone brands, so the exact words may differ. The button to look for is always some kind of Install or Add to Home screen.

Android phone or tablet

Chrome (most Android phones)

Best choice. Works on every recent Android.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Install app. On some versions it says Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install on the box that pops up.

How you know it worked: The Lineage Tracker tree icon appears on your home screen or in your app list. Tap it and the app opens full screen, with no address bar.

Samsung Internet (Samsung phones and tablets)

Works. The menu looks different to Chrome.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the menu (three lines, bottom right).
  3. Tap Add page to, then Home screen. If you see an Install icon in the address bar, you can tap that instead.
  4. Tap Add.

How you know it worked: The tree icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to open the app.

Firefox for Android

Works.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Firefox.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots).
  3. Tap Install (or Add to Home screen), then confirm.

How you know it worked: The tree icon appears on your home screen.

Edge for Android

Works. Wording varies a little between versions.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Edge.
  2. Tap the menu (three lines at the bottom).
  3. Tap Add to phone (or Add to Home screen), then confirm.

How you know it worked: The tree icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone and iPad

Safari

The most reliable way on Apple devices.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow. On iPhone it is at the bottom. On iPad it is at the top right.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

How you know it worked: The tree icon appears on your home screen. Open the app from that icon, not from Safari.

Chrome, Edge or Firefox on iPhone

Newer iPhones and iPads (iOS 16.4 or later) can add it from these too. If you can't find the option, use Safari.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in your browser.
  2. Tap the Share button or the menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

How you know it worked: The tree icon appears on your home screen. If there is no Add to Home Screen option, open the link in Safari and use the Safari steps.

Windows, Mac or Chromebook

Chrome

Works on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook. Watch the 48-second video of these steps on YouTube.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Chrome.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for a small install icon (a screen with a down arrow). Click it.
  3. If you can't see the icon: click the three dots menu, then Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app (older versions: Install Lineage Tracker).
  4. A box called Install app opens. Click Next (older versions say Install), then confirm on the next box if it asks.
  5. A box says Successfully installed. Click Open tab in app to start using it now, or Close and open it later from your desktop or Start menu.
  6. Later, to open it again: type Lineage in the search box next to the Start button and click Lineage Tracker. To keep it handy, right-click it and choose Pin to taskbar.

How you know it worked: Lineage Tracker opens in its own window and gets an icon on your desktop or in your Start menu or Applications folder.

Microsoft Edge

Works on Windows and Mac.

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (three little squares and a plus symbol). No icon? Open the three dots menu, then Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  3. A box called Install Lineage Tracker app opens. Click Install.
  4. Later, to open it again: type Lineage in the search box next to the Start button and click Lineage Tracker. To keep it handy, right-click it and choose Pin to taskbar.

How you know it worked: It opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu.

Safari on a Mac

Needs a recent Mac (macOS Sonoma or later).

  1. Open app.lineagetracker.org in Safari.
  2. Click File in the menu bar at the top of the screen, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.

How you know it worked: The app appears in your Dock. On an older Mac, just use Lineage Tracker in a normal tab, or use Chrome.

Firefox on a computer

Firefox on a computer does not offer install.

  1. Use Lineage Tracker in a normal Firefox tab. It works the same, but has no icon on your desktop.
  2. Want an icon? Use Chrome or Edge (steps above), or use the download option lower down this page.

How you know it worked: Your records are still saved on your device in the browser. Back up regularly.

It didn't work?
  • iPhone or iPad: use Safari. If there is no Add to Home Screen button, you are probably in an app's built-in browser. Copy the link into Safari.
  • No install button: make sure you opened the link in a normal browser window, not inside Facebook, Messenger or another app. Open the menu and choose Open in browser first.
  • Private or incognito window: installing is not offered there. Open a normal window.
  • Computer with Firefox: it can't install it. Use Chrome or Edge, or just use the app in a normal tab.
  • Still stuck? Use the feedback form and say which device and browser you have. We'll help.
Your records live on your device. Back up often with Settings, Back up now, and keep a copy somewhere else, like a USB stick or Google Drive.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, and it stays that way. The app is free forever: no subscription, no ads and no paid tier. Donations help keep the project going.

Does it need an account or internet?

No account. You need internet the first time you open it; after that it works offline. Weather and the optional smart features use the internet when you ask for them.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Optional sync keeps them in step through your own Google Drive or Dropbox, or you can move records with a backup file.

Is it for plants or animals?

Both, or either. You choose when you first open it, and it only shows what's relevant.

Who can see my records?

Only you. Records stay on your device. Nothing is shared unless you share it yourself, and Lineage Tracker never handles payments or adds personal details to anything you share.
