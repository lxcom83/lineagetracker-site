Support

Support Lineage Tracker

The app is free forever. Donations help keep it that way.

Lineage Tracker is an independent project built by a home breeder for home breeders. The app has no paid tiers, no ads and no locked features, and that is a commitment, not an introductory offer. Your records stay on your own device. Donations are welcome, never expected. If the website's optional online features ever need other income, we'll say so openly first, label it clearly, and keep it out of the app. If you never log in to the website, none of that affects you.

Donate on Ko-fi

Donations open soon. In the meantime, the best help is to try the app and tell us what you think.

What your support does

  • Keeps the app free forever for every gardener, seed saver and breeder, and helps cover the costs of running the website.
  • Pays for the time that goes into new features, fixes and help for users.
  • Brings it to more people, including app stores and the free versions for clubs and seed libraries.

Lineage Tracker is built and run independently. Donations go to its developer and aren't tax-deductible.

Other ways to help

  • Test it and report what breaks or confuses you. How to help test
  • Tell your seed-saving or breeding group about it.
  • Share seed with its history, using the gift file, so good varieties keep their story.
  • Suggest features that would help your plants or animals. Send an idea
